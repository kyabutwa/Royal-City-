# Royal City — Definition

## Canonical working definition

Royal City is an intelligent living infrastructure that connects people, communities, and the physical and economic world around them through one unified identity and ecosystem.

It connects identity, access, buildings, units, payments, economy, services, mobility, commerce, education, environment, and AI into a coordinated system for voluntarily participating communities.

At its core, Royal City gives people and authorized organizations a unified identity through which they can access places, use facilities, request and provide services, make payments, participate in commerce and community activities, interact with physical infrastructure, and perform actions across the ecosystem.

People and communities remain in control of what they authorize. Every action is governed by identity, permissions, relationships, context, and the rules of the relevant community or service.

## Fundamental idea

**One identity. One connected ecosystem. Any authorized action.**

## Foundational relationship

`Identity → Authorization → Action → Resource / Place / Service → Result → Record`

This document is a working foundation. Domain details that have not yet been explicitly established are intentionally left open rather than invented.
