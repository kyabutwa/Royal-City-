# Core Principles

## 1. Identity is the connective layer

Royal City uses identity as the common basis through which actors can participate across the ecosystem.

## 2. Identity is not permission

A unified identity does not imply unrestricted access. Authorization determines what an identity may do, where, when, under what conditions, and on whose behalf.

## 3. People and communities remain in control

Participation is voluntary, and authorization must remain governed by the participating person, organization, community, or other legitimate authority.

## 4. Actions are first-class

The system should be designed around meaningful actions and their outcomes, not around disconnected applications.

## 5. The physical and digital worlds are connected

Royal City is intended to connect digital identity and systems with places, facilities, resources, services, economic activity, and approved physical infrastructure.

## 6. Domain before implementation

The real-world model must be established before implementation details are treated as requirements.

## 7. No invented requirements

If a domain rule, capability, actor, relationship, or workflow has not been established, it remains an open question.

## 8. One ecosystem does not mean one monolith

A unified experience and identity model do not determine a particular internal technical architecture. Technical boundaries remain an architecture-stage decision.
