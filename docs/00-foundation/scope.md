# Scope

## In scope

The foundational work concerns the definition and modeling of Royal City as a living infrastructure and ecosystem, including:

- people and participating actors
- identity and roles
- relationships and authorization
- places, buildings, units, facilities, and resources
- services and operations
- actions and their results
- economic activity and transactions
- community participation and governance
- communication and information
- security and safety
- workflows and domain state
- the eventual digital ecosystem architecture

## Explicitly not fixed yet

This repository does not currently assume:

- a specific application list
- a specific database
- a specific programming language
- a specific cloud provider
- a specific blockchain or payment network
- a specific hardware platform
- a specific AI architecture
- a specific organizational or governance structure beyond what the domain definition establishes

These may become decisions later, after the domain and architecture work establishes the need for them.
