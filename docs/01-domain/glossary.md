# Royal City Glossary

This is the canonical glossary for terms used by the Royal City domain and architecture documentation.

Terms should be added only when their meaning has been explicitly established.

## Initial terms

### Identity
A unified representation through which an actor can participate in the Royal City ecosystem.

### Authorization
The determination of whether an identity is permitted to perform a particular action under specified conditions.

### Action
A meaningful operation performed by an authorized actor against or through a resource, place, service, facility, economic activity, or physical/digital system.

### Ecosystem
The connected Royal City environment in which identities, relationships, resources, actions, services, places, economic activity, and systems interact.
