# Royal City — Stage 1 Resolution Status

## Purpose

This document records what Stage 1 has actually resolved and what still requires explicit Royal City policy.

## Newly established by the current policy clarification

### People
- People are human participants.
- People receive a Royal City identity and account.
- Their participation is managed according to applicable roles, relationships, authority, and authorization.
- People may participate through a community or directly through Royal City-owned services.

### Communities
- A community is a participating residential property or real-estate environment.
- A community does not receive a person-style identity/account.
- A community receives onboarding credentials to connect its management environment to the Royal City Network Operating System.
- Royal City coordinates the participating community's management, people, services, places, utilities, economy, and related systems.

### Royal City
- Royal City is the Network Operating System itself.
- Royal City coordinates the participating ecosystem.
- Royal City can own/operate services available directly to people.
- A person therefore does not require community association to participate in Royal City.

## Still partially resolved

- Actor taxonomy: people, communities, and Royal City have foundational roles, but organization/service-provider and machine/system actor semantics remain open.
- Identity lifecycle: person identity/account and community onboarding credentials are established conceptually, but issuance, verification, recovery, suspension, revocation, and compromise rules remain open.
- Service model: direct Royal City services and community/provider services are established as participation paths, but service obligations and lifecycle remain open.
- External boundary: Royal City coordination versus ownership is established conceptually, but detailed authority boundaries remain open.

## Stage 1 gate

**Status: OPEN**

The current policy clarification materially advances Stage 1 and resolves the foundational distinction between:

**People → identity/account**

**Communities → onboarding credentials**

**Royal City → Network Operating System**

It does not yet resolve all Stage 1 policy decisions. Stage 2 architecture must remain blocked until the remaining domain-policy questions are explicitly resolved or deliberately declared outside Royal City's domain.
