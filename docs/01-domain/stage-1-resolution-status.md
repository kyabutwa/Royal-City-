# Royal City — Stage 1 Resolution Status

## Purpose

This document records what Stage 1 has actually resolved and what still requires an explicit Royal City policy decision.

## Resolved from the established Royal City model

### Identity
- Identity is the unified participation layer.
- Identity is distinct from permission.
- Identity identifies the actor associated with an action.

### Authorization
- Authorization is separate from identity.
- Authorization is contextual.
- Authorization requires an identifiable basis of authority.

### Action
- Action is the universal domain interaction mechanism.
- Meaningful actions are attributable.
- Actions operate against or through a target/context.
- Actions produce results and may change state.

### Relationships
- Relationships are first-class domain concepts.
- Relationships may provide authority, access, responsibility, representation, participation, service, delegation, or governance context.
- Relationship semantics cannot be inferred merely from technical relationships.

### Economy
- Transaction is distinct from action.
- Not every action is economic.
- Economic semantics require explicit transaction rules.

### Service
- Service is distinct from provider, request, authorization, execution, and result.

### State
- Domain state is governed by domain rules.
- Technical storage capabilities do not define valid domain state.

### Boundary
- External integration does not automatically create internal Royal City authority.

### Participation
- Royal City participation is voluntary subject to legitimate applicable authority.

## Not resolvable without explicit Royal City policy

The following cannot honestly be closed from the current principles alone:

- identity issuance and verification policy
- final actor taxonomy
- exact relationship taxonomy
- ownership/control rules
- authority precedence
- delegation rules
- community governance
- physical property/control rules
- service obligations
- economic settlement/finality rules
- record retention/correction rules
- emergency authority
- external authority boundary
