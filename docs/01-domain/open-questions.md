# Stage 1 Open Questions

This is the authoritative list of unresolved questions currently known from the foundation work.

## Identity

- What exactly constitutes a Royal City identity?
- What identity attributes are authoritative?
- Who can establish an identity?
- Can an actor have multiple identities or contexts?
- How are identity changes handled?

## Actors

- What is the complete actor taxonomy?
- Can organizations act directly?
- Can communities act directly?
- When can a device or autonomous system be an actor?

## Relationships

- Which relationships are first-class?
- Which relationships create authority?
- Which relationships create access?
- Who can create, modify, and revoke relationships?

## Places and resources

- What is the exact hierarchy between place, building, unit, facility, infrastructure, and resource?
- Who owns them?
- Who controls them?
- Can control and ownership differ?
- What are their lifecycles?

## Services

- What exactly is a service in Royal City?
- Who can provide one?
- Who can consume one?
- What constitutes a service commitment?
- What is the difference between a service and an action?

## Authorization

- What authority models are valid?
- How does delegation work?
- Can authorization be conditional?
- What happens when authority changes during an action?
- Which actions require multiple authorities?

## Economy

- What constitutes a transaction?
- Which economic relationships belong inside Royal City?
- Which remain external?
- What settlement and dispute semantics are required?

## Community

- What constitutes a community?
- What are community boundaries?
- How is membership established?
- How does community authority interact with individual authority?
- How are community rules created and changed?

## State and records

- What domain state must be authoritative?
- Which actions require records?
- What must be immutable?
- What can be corrected?
- What is the relationship between an event, action, transaction, and state?

## Governance

- Who can establish Royal City-wide rules?
- Who can establish community-specific rules?
- How do conflicting authorities resolve?
- What is the escalation model?

## Safety and exceptional authority

- What happens during emergencies?
- Can normal authorization be overridden?
- Who has emergency authority?
- What must be recorded when emergency authority is used?

## Boundary

- What is inside Royal City's domain?
- What is merely integrated?
- What remains externally authoritative?

## Principle

Until these questions are answered, they remain open. The implementation must not silently decide them.
