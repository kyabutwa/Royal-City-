# Royal City — Stage 1 Completion Gate

Stage 1 is complete only when the Royal City World & Domain Model is sufficiently defined that Stage 2 architecture can be designed without inventing domain requirements.

## Formalized

- Core vocabulary
- Actor and identity distinction
- Identity versus authorization
- Authority as a distinct concern
- Relationships as first-class concepts
- Action versus transaction
- Service versus request/action
- State and record concepts
- Foundational domain invariants
- Major unresolved policy areas

## Still requiring explicit Royal City decisions

- Final actor taxonomy
- Identity lifecycle
- Relationship taxonomy
- Ownership and control semantics
- Authority sources and precedence
- Delegation
- Community membership and governance
- Place hierarchy
- Resource taxonomy
- Service lifecycle
- Action taxonomy and lifecycle
- Economic boundary and transaction lifecycle
- Settlement, dispute, refund, and finality rules
- Record authority and retention
- Emergency authority
- External-domain boundary

## Gate

**Stage 1 remains open.**

The model is now formal enough to conduct the remaining domain decisions systematically. Stage 2 must not begin until those decisions are sufficiently resolved.

## Rule

No software implementation is allowed to silently answer an unresolved Stage 1 question.
