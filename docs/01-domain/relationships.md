# Domain Relationships

The Royal City domain is relationship-driven.

At foundation level, the central relationship chain is:

`Identity → Relationships → Authorization → Action → Result`

Other relationships between people, communities, places, services, resources, organizations, and economic activity must be explicitly modeled during Stage 1.

This document will become the authoritative map of those relationships.

## Rule

Do not infer a relationship merely because two concepts could logically be connected. Record it only when the Royal City domain establishes the relationship and its semantics.
