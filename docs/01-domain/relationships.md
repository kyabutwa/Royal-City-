# Royal City — Domain Relationships

The Royal City domain is relationship-driven.

Core chain:

Identity → Relationships/Participations → Authority → Authorization → Action → Result

## Established relationships

### Person → Community
A person can participate in one or more communities through their Royal City identity. The participation establishes contextual roles and authority. Community participation can end independently of the identity.

### Person → Royal City Service
A person can participate directly in Royal City-owned services without a community. Each service may define its participation/operational rules within Royal City policy.

### Community → Service Provider
A community can onboard multiple independent providers. Each community/provider relationship is separately governed. A provider can serve multiple communities.

### Person → Service Provider
The same Royal City identity can hold worker/service-provider participations with multiple providers.

### Principal → Delegate
Authority can be delegated to another participant within the authority held.

### Principal → System
A person, community, provider, or Royal City service can authorize an approved system to act on its behalf using the system's own credentials.

## Boundary
Additional relationships must not be inferred merely because they seem logically possible. Their semantics must be explicitly established.
