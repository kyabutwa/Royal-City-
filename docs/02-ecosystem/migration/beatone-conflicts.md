# Royal City — BeatOne Conflict Register

## B-001 — Product identity

**Source:** BeatOne/Zalagren repository

The repository currently identifies Zalagren as the user-facing product while retaining BeatOne as compatibility/history.

**Royal City resolution:** REPLACE.

Royal City remains the canonical product identity. Zalagren/BeatOne names do not become Royal City product semantics.

---

## B-002 — BeatCore as platform authority

BeatOne defines BeatCore as the canonical technical foundation.

**Royal City resolution:** MERGE.

BeatCore contracts become source material for Royal City technical contracts. They do not become a competing Royal City platform authority.

---

## B-003 — Community identity/account semantics

BeatOne's canonical Community is a first-class collective context and its identity architecture permits non-human identity kinds.

**Royal City resolution:** ADAPT.

Community management remains a participating environment connected through onboarding credentials. It does not become a person-style Royal City identity/account.

---

## B-004 — Generic non-human identity

BeatOne supports identity kinds including human, organization, service and system.

**Royal City resolution:** ADAPT / OPEN.

Royal City already recognizes systems and providers as distinct participants/credential classes, but the complete non-person identity taxonomy remains open.

Do not close that gap merely by copying BeatOne's enum.

---

## B-005 — Place hierarchy

BeatOne has a narrower Build Phase → Building → Floor → Unit → Resource hierarchy.

**Royal City resolution:** MERGE / ADAPT.

Use BeatOne implementation patterns, but Royal City's richer place hierarchy remains canonical.

---

## B-006 — Payment authorization

BeatOne's payment contract uses generic BeatCore authorization.

Royal City has stricter payment-specific decisions:

- every payment requires explicit payer authorization;
- payer, not delegate, authorizes payment;
- payment authorization is scoped/versioned/revocable/expirable;
- external settlement remains external.

**Royal City resolution:** ADAPT.

BeatOne payment mechanics are reusable only after applying Royal City's payment-specific rules.

---

## B-007 — Payment production evidence

BeatOne contains production reconciliation material for its payment persistence and reports a verified production persistence boundary.

**Royal City resolution:** KEEP as source evidence; do not import production status.

Royal City must independently establish its own persistence and production verification.

---

## B-008 — GENESIS/intelligence

BeatOne keeps GENESIS behind authorization and proposal boundaries.

**Royal City resolution:** KEEP / MERGE.

This is highly compatible with Royal City's intelligence architecture and resource-forecasting authorization rule.

---

## B-009 — OneApp / Platform Website

BeatOne treats OneApp and Website as alternate experiences over shared canonical state.

**Royal City resolution:** ADAPT.

Royal City should have multiple applications/workspaces, but no experience surface becomes a parallel domain authority.

---

## B-010 — Kenya-first jurisdiction

BeatOne contains Kenya launch/compliance architecture.

**Royal City resolution:** OPEN / ADAPT.

Kenya-specific compliance material is valuable implementation input, but jurisdiction and regulatory applicability must be established as a Royal City legal/compliance workstream rather than silently becoming a global architectural assumption.
